B) False

Ans : False

B) leading a church in Galatia
C) eating with the gentiles
D) circumcision of gentiles

Ans: C) eating with the Gentiles
Ref: Gal 2:11-14

Q5: Christ redeemed us from the ________ of the Law.

A) power
B) bondage
C) curse
D) works

Ans : C) curse

Q6: The ultimate purpose of the Law is to lead us to_________
